I live in Arizona with my wife and kids. I trained as a molecular biologist and ended up building software.

These days, I help companies figure out what to build and lead the teams that build it. I'm also building with AI myself and helping teams work out where it's useful. Before this, I co-founded Myndshft, which DrFirst acquired in 2024.

A lot of what I make starts with something I want to keep: an idea from a book, something I've learned, a funny thing one of my kids said. You'll find some of those projects here, along with my writing and book notes.
