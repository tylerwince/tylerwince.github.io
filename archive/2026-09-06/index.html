Tyler Wince

I live in Arizona with my wife and kids. I'm a molecular biologist by training and started my career in research and teaching before finding my way into software.

In 2016, I co-founded Myndshft, where we built software to automate the paperwork that gets between patients and care. DrFirst acquired the company in 2024, and I joined to lead product and technology.

Today, I work with companies as a product and technology leader. I spend much of my time building with AI and helping teams rethink how they build software.

I also make apps for things I care about: Etch for remembering what I learn, Waymark for deciding what I'm formed by, and Little Quotes for the things my kids say that I never want to forget.

You can find me on X or shoot me an email.
